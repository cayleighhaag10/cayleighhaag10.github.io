Work at Boeing (Summer 2025)

This presentation summarizes my contributions as a Software Engineering Intern at The Boeing Company (Summer 2025). I improved the C-17 flight deck screen testing tool, Playback, by implementing feature enhancements and refactoring components of the existing codebase (hosted on GitHub). These changes were pushed for long-term use in the C-17 engineering team’s workflow. I also designed and implemented a spatial hashing algorithm to condense data for C-17 Multifunctional Displays, improving data organization and usability. Additionally, I helped lead the transition of four engineering teams to Jira, coordinating the migration and supporting adoption alongside senior C-17 project managers.
